About the Game

Goose Patrol is set on the University of York's Campus East, shown from a top-down perspective. The map is split into segments, and only one is visible at a time. Geese roam each area until you come within range, then chase you to block your movement, and more of them target you as the game goes on. Collide with one and you lose health; drop to zero and it is game over. You start paused with the controls on screen and five minutes on the timer. Win by dispersing enough geese or reaching the final end location, and the victory screen shows your final score and statistics.

Features

Campus East, segment by segment

Reach the edge of an area and the next one loads in its place. The geese and items left behind are cleared, while your health, score and inventory carry over.

Bread and traps

Items are scattered around the map for you to pick up. Bread lures a goose over and holds it while it feeds; a trap stops one for five seconds, after which it leaves campus.

The boss goose

Hidden events fire when you enter a new area, flagged by a banner on the HUD. One of them is the boss goose in the final segment, a greater challenge than an ordinary goose.
